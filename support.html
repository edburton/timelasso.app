Timelasso Support

Questions, problems or a video you’re pleased with: email hello@timelasso.app. Timelasso is made by Ed Burton.

Long recordings

Plug the phone in for anything over an hour or so, and stand it on a tripod or something steady. Keep a few gigabytes free: a minute of 4K video needs about 2–3 GB while it records and saves.

Leave Timelasso on screen while it records. If you switch to another app, or the phone gets too hot, the recording stops and you can still save what it had.

Short recordings

When you choose a length, Timelasso shows the shortest recording that length needs. Stop before then and the video is sped up rather than blended.

Remove Watermark

Remove Watermark is a one-time purchase. Buy it from Settings (Imprint, then Watermark Off) or from a finished video. The same dialog has Restore Purchase, for a new device or after reinstalling.

Camera and Photos access

If Timelasso can’t use the camera or save to Photos, turn access on in the Settings app under Apps, then Timelasso.

If a recording goes wrong

If a recording stops early, won’t save, or the phone gets hot, please email the newest file from Files → On My iPhone → Timelasso (On My iPad on an iPad) whose name starts with TimePress. It’s a small log of numbers, with no pictures or video, and it’s the only way to see what happened: nothing is sent automatically.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later.

Privacy
